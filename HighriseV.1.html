First Alarm Assignments & Staging

First Alarm Company Assignments

1st Arriving District Chief

Assignment & Location:
Incident Command Post (CP)
Key Tasks:
Establish Command with a clearly announced location, request additional resources (2nd Alarm on confirmation), and maintain accountability.

1st Arriving Engine

Assignment & Location:
Fire Floor (Crew) & Floor Below (Driver/Operator)
Key Tasks:
Entire crew proceeds to fire floor for attack. Driver/Operator stays on floor below (at standpipe outlet) to control flow and pressure (psi) for the attack line.

1st Arriving Truck

Assignment & Location:
Fire Floor (Crew) or Aerial Operation
Key Tasks:
Entire crew reports to the fire floor (if no aerial operation needed). Tasks include: Forcible Entry, Locate Fire, Search and Rescue, Ventilation.

1st Arriving Squad

Assignment & Location:
Lobby Control
Key Tasks:
Establish Lobby Control, announce accountability location, secure elevators (Phase I recall), prepare to pressurize stairwells using PPV, and control the fire alarm system/building systems.

2nd Arriving Engine

Assignment & Location:
Fire Floor (Crew) & Ground Level (Driver/Operator)
Key Tasks:
Driver/Operator establishes the Water Supply to the FDC and remains at the pump panel. The crew reports to the fire floor to assist the 1st Engine with hose deployment and establishing the backup line. Bring: High-rise pack, bag, hand tools.

2nd Arriving Truck

Assignment & Location:
Floor Above Fire
Key Tasks:
Position opposite 1st Truck (if no aerial needed). Crew reports to the floor above the fire to support 3rd Engine's search, ventilation, and exposure control. (If fire is at the top floor, report to IC).

3rd Engine

Assignment & Location:
Floor Above Fire
Key Tasks:
Crew proceeds to the floor above the fire to control vertical fire spread and auto-exposure, and initiate primary search of that floor.

4th Engine (RIC)

Assignment & Location:
Rapid Intervention Crew (RIC)
Key Tasks:
Report to the floor below the fire floor to stage as the dedicated RIC. Bring appropriate RIC equipment bag and tools.

Alarm Levels & Staging Locations

1st Alarm

Composition: 4 Engines, 2 Trucks, 1 Squad, 2 District Chiefs (+ optional 1 Rescue or 1 LR)

Staging:

Respond directly to scene/CP

2nd Alarm or Greater

Composition: 3 Engines, 1 Truck, 1 Squad, 1 District Chief

Staging:

Report to Staging (2 floors below fire)

3rd Alarm or Greater

Composition: ---

Staging:

Report to Base (min. 200 ft from building)

RIC Staging

Composition: Dedicated RIC (4th Engine)

Staging:

1 floor below the fire floor

Staging Floor (Level II)

Composition: All uncommitted 2nd Alarm+ resources

Staging:

2 floors below the fire floor

Base Manager

Composition: IC Assignment (often 1st arriving officer from additional alarm)

Location:

Manages check-in at Base
